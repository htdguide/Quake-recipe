# gnu.txt

> Data: the licence the source is released under.

**Needs** — nothing
**Used by** — the reader
**Tier floor** — none

## Purpose

The full text of the licence. It is legal content, reproduced verbatim in the source and not summarized here.

## State

Data; no run-time state.

## What it records

The terms under which the engine source may be used, modified and redistributed, and the requirement that derived works carry the same
terms.

**Notes** — the recipe records its existence and does not paraphrase it. **A rebuilder should read the original text**, because the
obligations it creates are a real constraint on how a rebuild derived from this source may be distributed, and a summary would be both
inaccurate and unhelpful. Note also that this recipe describes the source's *design*; whether a reimplementation written from it is a
derived work is a question for the rebuilder and not one this page can answer.
